## 3. 需求目标

在对时序数据进行 `session`、`state`、`event` 窗口计算时，针对第一个窗口和最后一个窗口，存在以下四个关键时间点：
1. `ts-start`：通常为数据筛选条件的起始时间
2. `ts-end`：通常为数据筛选条件的结束时间
3. `ts-start-real`：第一个窗口的实际开始时间
4. `ts-end-real`：最后一个窗口的实际结束时间
为 `session`、`state`、`event` 窗口新增语法选项，用于输出最外侧两个窗口的实际起止时间。建议语法如下
```sql {wrap}
window_clause: {
    SESSION(ts_col, tol_val) WINBOUND( 0 | 1 | 2)
  | STATE_WINDOW(col) WINBOUND( 0 | 1 | 2)
  | EVENT_WINDOW START WITH …… END WITH …… WINBOUND( 0 | 1 | 2)
}
```

新增关键字 `WINBOUND`，用于指定窗口边界的扩展类型：
1. 取值 `0`：默认值，与现有行为相同，输出 `ts-start`, `ts-end`
2. 取值 `1`：向外扩展，寻找最外侧两个窗口的实际时间，输出 `ts-start-real`, `ts-end-real`
3. 取值 `2`：向内收缩，忽略最外侧的两个窗口（尚未有用户提出此类需求，先不定义和实现）

## 4. 功能需求

### 4.1 会话窗口边界扩展

1. 原始数据
```sql {wrap}
taos> select ts, v from tb;

           ts            |      v      |
========================================
 2019-04-28 14:22:10.000 |           1 |
 2019-04-28 14:22:20.000 |           2 |
 2019-04-28 14:22:30.000 |           3 |
 2019-04-28 14:23:10.000 |           4 |
 2019-04-28 14:23:20.000 |           5 |
 2019-04-28 14:23:30.000 |           6 |
 2019-04-28 14:24:10.000 |           7 |
 2019-04-28 14:24:20.000 |           8 |
 2019-04-28 14:24:30.000 |           9 |
```

1. 当前结果
```sql {wrap}
taos> SELECT _wstart, count(*), first(v), last(v) FROM tb 
      WHERE ts BETWEEN '2019-04-28 14:22:20.000' AND '2019-04-28 14:24:20.000'
      SESSION(ts, 30s);

         _wstart         |       count(*)        |  first(v)   |   last(v)   |
==============================================================================
 2019-04-28 14:22:20.000 |                     2 |           2 |           3 |
 2019-04-28 14:23:10.000 |                     3 |           4 |           6 |
 2019-04-28 14:24:10.000 |                     2 |           7 |           8 |
```

1. 期待结果
```sql {wrap}
taos> SELECT _wstart, count(*), first(v), last(v) FROM tb 
      WHERE ts BETWEEN '2019-04-28 14:22:20.000' AND '2019-04-28 14:23:20.000'
      SESSION(ts, 30s)
      WINBOUND(1);

         _wstart         |       count(*)        |  first(v)   |   last(v)   |
==============================================================================
 2019-04-28 14:22:10.000 |                     3 |           1 |           3 |
 2019-04-28 14:23:10.000 |                     3 |           4 |           6 |
 2019-04-28 14:24:10.000 |                     3 |           7 |           9 |
```

### 4.2 事件窗口边界扩展

1. 原始数据
```sql {wrap}
taos> select ts, c1, c2 from tb;
           ts            |     c1      |     c2      |
======================================================
 2023-02-23 18:31:00.000 |           1 |          15 |
 2023-02-23 18:31:01.000 |           1 |          11 |
 2023-02-23 18:31:02.000 |          -2 |           9 |
 2023-02-23 18:31:04.000 |          -4 |           8 |
 2023-02-23 18:31:05.000 |           2 |          17 |
 2023-02-23 18:31:06.000 |          -5 |          15 |
 2023-02-23 18:31:07.000 |          -1 |          12 |
 2023-02-23 18:31:08.000 |          -1 |           3 |
 2023-02-23 18:31:09.000 |          10 |          17 |
 2023-02-23 18:31:10.000 |           5 |          18 |
```

1. 当前结果
```sql {wrap}
taos> SELECT _wstart, count(*), first(c1), last(c1) FROM tb 
      WHERE ts BETWEEN '2023-02-23 18:31:01.000' AND '2023-02-23 18:31:07.000'
      event_window start with c1 > 0 end with c2 < 10;

         _wstart         |       count(*)        |  first(c1)  |  last(c1)   |
==============================================================================
 2023-02-23 18:31:01.000 |                     2 |           1 |          -2 |
```

1. 期待结果
关于最后一个窗口未满足结束条件时是否应输出，需在开发阶段进一步讨论确定
```sql {wrap}
taos> SELECT _wstart, count(*), first(c1), last(c1) FROM tb 
      WHERE ts BETWEEN '2023-02-23 18:31:01.000' AND '2023-02-23 18:31:07.000'
      event_window start with c1 > 0 end with c2 < 10;
      WINBOUND(1);
    
         _wstart         |       count(*)        |  first(c1)  |  last(c1)   |
==============================================================================
 2023-02-23 18:31:00.000 |                     3 |           1 |          -2 |
 2023-02-23 18:31:05.000 |                     4 |           2 |          -1 |
```

### 4.3 状态窗口边界扩展

1. 原始数据
```sql {wrap}
taos> select ts, stat from tb;
           ts            |    stat     |
========================================
 2019-04-28 14:22:07.000 |           1 |
 2019-04-28 14:22:08.000 |           1 |
 2019-04-28 14:22:09.000 |           1 |
 2019-04-28 14:22:10.000 |           1 |
 2019-04-28 14:22:11.000 |           0 |
 2019-04-28 14:22:12.000 |           0 |
```

1. 当前结果
```sql {wrap}
taos>  SELECT _wstart, count(*), first(stat), last(stat) FROM tb 
       WHERE ts BETWEEN '2019-04-28 14:22:08.000' AND '2019-04-28 14:22:11.000'
       STATE_WINDOW(stat);

         _wstart         |       count(*)        | first(stat) | last(stat)  |
==============================================================================
 2019-04-28 14:22:08.000 |                     3 |           1 |           1 |
 2019-04-28 14:22:11.000 |                     1 |           0 |           0 |
```

1. 期待结果
最后一个窗口未满足 end 条件，是否应该输出，需要开发时再讨论确定
```sql {wrap}
taos> SELECT _wstart, count(*), first(stat), last(stat) FROM tb 
      WHERE ts BETWEEN '2019-04-28 14:22:08.000' AND '2019-04-28 14:22:11.000'
      STATE_WINDOW(stat)
      WINBOUND(1);

         _wstart         |       count(*)        | first(stat) | last(stat)  |
==============================================================================
 2019-04-28 14:22:07.000 |                     4 |           1 |           1 |
 2019-04-28 14:22:11.000 |                     2 |           0 |           0 |
```
