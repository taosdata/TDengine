TDengine IDMP is an AI-native industrial data management platform. Based on collected data, it can automatically sense application scenarios, generate dashboards and reports without prompting, perform real-time analytics, and detect anomalies—giving you an industrial intelligence agent for dependency-free real-time decisions.

:::tip

- **[Why TDengine IDMP](https://tdengine.com/idmp/)**: Use LLM-driven proactive visualization and real-time analytics, or ask questions in natural language or by voice, as a full-stack industrial data solution.

- **[TDengine IDMP Documentation](https://idmpdocs.taosdata.com/en/)**: From product concepts to basic and advanced features, operations and deployment, plus best practices across scenarios.

- **[TDengine IDMP Use Cases](https://tdengine.com/idmp/)**: From collection-side configuration, writing data to TDengine, to IDMP asset trees, visualization, and real-time analytics.

:::
